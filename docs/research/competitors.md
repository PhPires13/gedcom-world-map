# Competitor landscape: Luso-Brazilian heritage service

As of: 2026-11-10

Method: every page cited was opened (WebFetch or an in-app browser tab). Search results were used only to discover URLs. Prices are quoted exactly in the currency shown, and "seen" means 2026-11-10 unless another date is given. Many pages were opened from a Brazil IP, so Etsy and MyHeritage displayed BRL. Items marked UNVERIFIED could not be opened or read.

Legal context, because it changes who is eligible (all from pages opened):
- Italy: one 2025 article says the applicant "must be a child or grandchild" of someone with exclusively Italian citizenship, and great-grandchildren lose the administrative route, with judicial attempts still possible but uncertain ([Euro Dicas, 18 Jun 2025](https://www.eurodicas.com.br/cidadania-italiana-para-netos-e-bisnetos/)). La Via Italia says the same on its service page ([La Via Italia](https://laviaitalia.com.br/servicos/reconheca-a-sua-cidadania-italiana)).
- Portugal: grandchildren can still apply, but a 2026 law adds requirements (A2 Portuguese, proof of knowledge of Portuguese culture/history; implementing regulations were still pending when the page was updated) ([Atlantic Bridge](https://blog.atlanticbridge.com.br/en/portuguese-citizenship-for-grandchildren-of-portuguese)). Cidadania e Visto states the 2026 law revoked the Sephardic basis and that great-grandchildren have no dedicated route ([Cidadania e Visto](https://www.cidadaniaevisto.com.br/blog/quanto-custa-o-processo-de-cidadania-portuguesa-em-2026-guia-completo-de-valores)). These are commercial sources, not statutes. Check the law before any marketing claim.

## Analysis

### Where the white space is

1. **Relationship-level evidence with a confidence rating, offered to consumers.** Of the tools I could open, only FamilySearch demonstrably lets users attach sources to a parent-child relationship, and it does so in a volunteer-edited shared tree without confidence grades. Vestigee promises "citations" with no visible relationship-level view. Ancestry and MyHeritage behaviour on relationships is UNVERIFIED. No print or research seller shows per-link confidence.
2. **A data-driven antique-style migration print with flags and routes, derived from the customer's own tree and Luso-Brazilian records.** The existing physical offers are generic templates (TheContourPress, Mapshop), blank fill-in templates (Vestigee) or tree charts (Family Chart Masters, MyCanvas). The one adjacent offer that does tie each fact to a source (TruescaleMaps) is a brand-new shop, digital-only, and US-record oriented.
3. **Luso-Brazilian specificity.** No platform page I opened names Portuguese, Brazilian or Italian parish and civil collections beyond FamilySearch's wiki lists. Citizenship firms sell the *documents* (certificates, apostille, translation), not an inspectable evidence graph. Several explicitly separate "genealogical research" from "certificate search" (Simonato) or leave the deliverable undefined (Atlantic Bridge, Cidadania e Visto).
4. **Gap between a R$650 to R$2,000 certificate search and a R$10,000+ full process.** An evidence pack that makes a family line reviewable by the customer's own lawyer or consulate sits in between, and no firm advertises that deliverable by name.
5. **Rule volatility is a service opportunity and a risk.** Rules changed in 2025 (Italy) and 2026 (Portugal) according to the commercial pages above. A pack must carry a visible "as of" date for eligibility rules and must not promise approvals.
