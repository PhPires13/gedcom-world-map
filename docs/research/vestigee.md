# Vestigee: competitor profile

As of: 2026-11-10

Method: pages were opened with WebFetch and, where the site renders client-side, an in-app browser tab (Brazil IP address). Prices are quoted as displayed on 2026-11-10. Search snippets were used only to find URLs. Nothing below is cited from a snippet.

## 1. Which "Vestigee" is it?

- The only match I could open is **vestigee.com**, an AI genealogy platform plus a small print shop. The legal notice names the operator as **Vestige SA, Lausanne, Switzerland** (UID CHE-167.452.034) and a US entity, **Vestigee Inc, Dover, Delaware** ([Legal notice](https://www.vestigee.com/impressum)). The Terms of Service (last updated 6 November 2026) say US residents contract with Vestigee Inc. ([Terms](https://www.vestigee.com/terms-of-service)).
- Ambiguity: my web search for "Vestigee family tree heritage map print" returned nothing about Vestigee at all. I found the site by fetching the domain directly. I cannot rule out other businesses with a similar name. The match with the plan owner's description ("similar service") rests on vestigee.com being the only one found.

## 2. What it is

- An **AI-led genealogy subscription**: tree builder, record search ("40 billion records"), AI document reading (OCR/translation), AI photo restoration/colorization/animation, biographies, and AI "genealogist" suggestions ([home](https://www.vestigee.com/), [Data sources](https://www.vestigee.com/data-sources), [Pricing](https://www.vestigee.com/pricing)).
- Plus a **print shop** that sells **fill-in-by-hand** family tree posters, research notebooks and guided journals ([Shop](https://www.vestigee.com/shop)). It does **not** sell personalised map prints or migration-route prints. The shop's three products are fill-in posters, notebooks and journals.
- Self-reported scale: 500,000+ users, 159 archives/collections, 45+ countries, 850M+ photos ([home](https://www.vestigee.com/)). These are marketing claims I could not verify.

## 4. How it builds trees and records

- **GEDCOM upload: yes.** The GEDCOM page says you drop a `.ged`/`.gedcom` file and it parses individuals, families, events, sources and media links. It then merges the import with "AI record matching" ([GEDCOM page](https://www.vestigee.com/gedcom)). The Terms also promise GEDCOM export ([Terms s.2, s.14](https://www.vestigee.com/terms-of-service)).
- **AI from records: yes, claimed.** The site says the AI searches census, civil, parish, military and migration records and checks a match against your tree before suggesting a link. You then "approve or dismiss" ([Data sources](https://www.vestigee.com/data-sources)). The "vs Ancestry" page says it "proposes sourced ancestors with an explanation of the reasoning" ([vs Ancestry](https://www.vestigee.com/ancestry)).
- **AI document reading: yes.** Upload a photo or scan and the AI extracts names, dates, places and relationships. The page claims "99.8% accuracy on printed text" and "strong results on cursive". I could not verify either figure ([Data sources](https://www.vestigee.com/data-sources)).
- **Map:** the "Time Travel" feature generates a narrative and an illustration for a chosen year and town, with an interactive map. This is a storytelling feature, not a tree-driven migration map ([Time Travel](https://www.vestigee.com/features/time-travel)). Vestigee's own Trustpilot company blurb mentions visualising "life journeys" in a map view ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/vestigee.com)). I could not see an in-app map view because it sits behind login.

## 5. Print quality, sizes, formats

- Poster sizes: A3 and A2 prints; "A3, framed" with a classic wood frame in natural wood, black or white, snow-white mount and acrylic glazing; "print it yourself" HD vector PDF in A3 and A2 ([Poster page](https://www.vestigee.com/shop/family-tree-poster)).
- Paper: 200gsm enhanced matte art paper. The notebook uses uncoated 120gsm paper ([Poster page](https://www.vestigee.com/shop/family-tree-poster), [Notebook page](https://www.vestigee.com/shop/research-notebook)).
- Fulfilment: print on demand "in the lab closest to you, in Europe, the UK, the US or Canada", 2-5 business days to print, then tracked shipping (standard usually 3-5 days). Reprint at no cost if damaged or misprinted, with a photo, within 30 days ([Poster page](https://www.vestigee.com/shop/family-tree-poster), [Shop](https://www.vestigee.com/shop)).
- Content: **generic template** with 31 oval frames over five generations and a family name in the title. The customer fills in names and photos **by hand** ([Poster page](https://www.vestigee.com/shop/family-tree-poster)). Nothing is generated from the user's tree data. The shop does say users who have a tree can sign in for "tees, prints and gifts made from your own tree and photos" ([Shop](https://www.vestigee.com/shop)). I could not see that flow.
- No antique styling, flags or routes are offered on any shop page I opened.

## 6. Markets, shipping, languages

- The site has **32 language versions**, listed in the homepage hreflang tags, including `pt-BR` (/pt), `it-IT` (/it), fr, de, es, nl, pl and ja ([home](https://www.vestigee.com/)).
- Shop shipping claims "tracked delivery worldwide" with print labs in Europe, the UK, the US and Canada. I did not find a country list, so shipping to Brazil is **UNVERIFIED** ([Shop](https://www.vestigee.com/shop)).
- Self-described depth: "deepest coverage in France, Western Europe and North America" ([vs Ancestry FAQ](https://www.vestigee.com/ancestry)). The data catalog is France-shaped: "Death Notices", "Departmental/Regional Archives (1,284 services)", name-origin databases ([Data sources](https://www.vestigee.com/data-sources)). The comparison pages target Geneanet, Filae and Archion, which are Franco-German brands ([home](https://www.vestigee.com/)).

## 7. Evidence, citation and confidence features

- Claims: "source citations so you can verify the information yourself", user edit/verify/correct, "AI fact verification" and "real-time checking" ([Pricing](https://www.vestigee.com/pricing), [GEDCOM page FAQ](https://www.vestigee.com/gedcom)).
- The Terms warn that AI output "are not historical records" and may be inaccurate. Genealogical records, matches and AI content should be checked against original sources ([Terms s.7, s.11](https://www.vestigee.com/terms-of-service)).
- I found **no per-relationship confidence level, evidence grading or "proof" view** on any public page. Whether source citations attach to each relationship, as opposed to each person, is **UNVERIFIED**. It would need a logged-in account.

## 8. Portugal, Brazil, Italy coverage

- No public page names a Portuguese, Brazilian or Italian record collection. The data-sources catalog lists generic categories (civil, census, migrations, parish) with no country breakdown ([Data sources](https://www.vestigee.com/data-sources)).
- The Portuguese-language home page uses sample cards ("Isabel Silva, Porto, Portugal", "José Santos, Lisboa") but these are demo content and not evidence of coverage ([PT home](https://www.vestigee.com/pt)).
- Its own FAQ says uploads of scans from "a local archive" go through the same AI pipeline, so users are "never limited to our index" ([vs Ancestry](https://www.vestigee.com/ancestry)). This implies that for Luso-Brazilian records the user supplies the documents.
- A Trustpilot reviewer from Canada says it "doesn't find records that I know exist". This is one anecdote ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/vestigee.com)).
- No citizenship-by-descent feature appears on any page I opened.

## 9. Reviews

- **Trustpilot: 4.0 / 925 reviews** (65% 5-star, 8% 4-star, 3% 3-star, 1% 2-star, 23% 1-star). The profile was claimed in Dec 2025, and the page shows a "Paid Trustpilot subscription" badge and the company replying to 82% of negative reviews ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/vestigee.com)).
- Positive themes: fast support (an AI-assisted agent named "Lily" is mentioned by name by several reviewers), easy to use, photo colorization works.
- Negative themes in the 1-star filter: the 5-day trial converting into a paid subscription (charges of about £40 to £43 reported), refund friction, cancellation confusion, results seen as thin or inaccurate (one reviewer says the report put a deceased grandfather's birth in 1901 and only gave surname information), and "no records found" ([Trustpilot 1-star filter](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/vestigee.com?stars=1)). The company reply on those reviews points to the trial auto-conversion terms.
- Discrepancy: the site's homepage claims "4.9/5 based on 12,847 reviews" and the pricing page says "Rated 2,847 reviews" ([home](https://www.vestigee.com/), [Pricing](https://www.vestigee.com/pricing)). The only third-party figure I could open (Trustpilot) is 4.0 on 925 reviews. The site's own /reviews page describes itself as "hand-picked" Trustpilot opinions ([Reviews](https://www.vestigee.com/reviews)).
- The pricing-page comparison lists Ancestry at "$49.99" a month; Ancestry's own page that day showed World Explorer at USD39.99/mo ([Vestigee Pricing](https://www.vestigee.com/pricing), [Ancestry](https://www.ancestry.com/offers/subscribe)). The comparison is marketing, not a neutral reference.

## 10. Strengths and weaknesses versus the plan

| Plan element | Vestigee | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence-based tree, relationship-level documents plus confidence | Source citations and "fact verification" claimed; no visible relationship-level evidence or confidence grading | Open ground; Vestigee does not publicly show it |
| AI that reads old parish/civil records and proposes links to confirm | Yes: OCR/translation of uploads, AI hints the user approves or dismisses ([Data sources](https://www.vestigee.com/data-sources)) | **Direct overlap.** Vestigee is a mass-market, trial-led, 40B-record product with France-centred catalog |
| Interactive migration map | Not found as a product; "Time Travel" is narrative + map | Plan is ahead |
| Framed antique-style heritage map print with flags and routes | Not offered; only fill-in tree posters/notebooks | Plan is ahead |
| Research packages and citizenship evidence packs | Not offered | Plan is ahead |
| Luso-Brazilian depth | None shown; pt-BR UI only | Plan is ahead |
| Pricing posture | One all-in plan, low-priced trial with auto-conversion, high complaint share about billing | Plan can win on transparency |
| Distribution | 32 languages, print labs in EU/UK/US/Canada, 500K claimed users, paid Trustpilot profile | Vestigee has reach and ad spend the plan lacks |

Vestigee strengths: broad AI toolset in one subscription; GEDCOM in and out; localisation into Brazilian Portuguese and Italian UI; global print-on-demand logistics; low entry price.
Vestigee weaknesses: no evidence of Portuguese, Brazilian or Italian record collections; no per-link confidence; print line is generic fill-in templates, not data-driven maps; billing-trust complaints; inflated or inconsistent social-proof numbers; prices on the shop vary between page loads.

## 11. What this service should do differently

1. **Make evidence the product, not a footnote.** Show for each parent-child and marriage link the documents behind it, the transcription, and a stated confidence level with the reason. Vestigee's public pages promise "citations" but show nothing at relationship level. FamilySearch supports sources on parent-child relationships ([FamilySearch help](https://articles.familysearch.org/hc/en-us/articles/41581468130715-How-do-I-attach-a-source-to-a-parent-child-relationship)), so the claim needs to be stronger than "has sources".
2. **Own Luso-Brazilian records, in Portuguese.** Name specific collections (parish registers, civil registration, immigration cards) and be explicit about gaps. Vestigee's pages stay generic and France-weighted.
3. **Sell the data-driven map print, which Vestigee does not.** Vestigee prints blank templates. A print generated from the customer's own migration data, with flags and routes, is a different product. Price transparently, since Vestigee's shop prices moved between loads.
4. **Do not copy the billing model.** The recurring complaint is trial-to-paid conversion. Use one-off packages and an explicit monthly plan with a clear first-charge date and one-click cancel.
5. **Keep AI proposals reviewable.** Keep "AI proposes, human confirms" as the visible workflow and publish accuracy limits. Vestigee's own Terms disclaim that AI outputs are not historical records, while its marketing cites 99.8% OCR accuracy.
6. **Add what a mass-market tool cannot: human-reviewed packages and citizenship evidence packs.** These are absent from Vestigee. Cite current eligibility rules and avoid promising outcomes (see competitors.md for the legal-change context).
7. **Use no inflated trust numbers.** Show real review counts from a third-party site.
